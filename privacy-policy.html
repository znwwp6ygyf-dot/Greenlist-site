Greenlist Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Greenlist ("the app", "we", "us") is a golf course tracking app that lets you log the courses you've played, keep a wishlist, and share your rounds with friends. This page explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Account information. When you sign up, we collect your email address and a password. Your password is handled by our authentication provider (Supabase) and is never stored or visible to us in plain text.

Profile information you provide. Username, display name, bio, handicap, home course, profile photo, and up to four favorite courses — all optional and editable at any time in the Profile tab.

Activity you log. Courses you mark as played or add to your wishlist, along with any date, star rating, notes, or photos you choose to attach to a round.

Social activity. Who you follow, who follows you, and any likes or comments you post on rounds.

Location. With your permission, the app uses your device's location to sort the course list by distance when you're adding courses. This location is used only on your device for that sorting and is not stored on our servers.

We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking software of any kind, and we do not sell your data to anyone.

How your information is shared with other users

Greenlist has social features, so some of your information is visible to other people by design:

  • Your username, display name, bio, profile photo, and public profile details are visible to other users.
  • Rounds you log through the map or quick-add are visible to people who follow you (course, date, rating, and any notes or photos attached). Rounds added via the onboarding bulk-add tool are kept out of the shared feed.
  • Comments and likes you post on a round are visible to anyone who can see that round.

We never share your email address or password with other users.

Where your information is stored

Greenlist is built on Supabase, which hosts our database, handles authentication, and stores any photos you upload. Map tiles are provided by Apple Maps (iOS); no personal data is sent to Apple beyond what's needed to display the map. App updates are delivered via Expo's EAS Update service, which does not have access to your account data.

Your choices and control

  • You can edit or remove your profile information, rounds, wishlist entries, photos, follows, likes, and comments at any time within the app.
  • You can request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us using the details below.

Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, your data is permanently removed within a reasonable time.

Children's privacy

Greenlist is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
